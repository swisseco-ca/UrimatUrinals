# UrimatUrinals.ca | Codex master build prompt
Version 1 • 21 October 2026

Act as a senior website designer, developer and Canadian B2B content strategist. Build UrimatUrinals.ca into a bilingual education and lead-generation platform for URIMAT waterless urinals, supplied by Swiss Eco Media. Work autonomously on reversible implementation. Preserve existing production hosting, DNS and customer data until a migration is explicitly requested. Create a private review build first. Inspect existing project identity before creating a new project.

## Objective and audience
Help Canadian facility owners understand waterless urinals, assess their building, estimate water and operating cost differences, and prepare a qualified inquiry. Serve facility managers, property managers, schools, universities, arenas, restaurants, malls, airports, architects and contractors. This site's role is dedicated URIMAT education and conversion. Use original copy distinct from Swiss Eco Media, WaterlessUrinals.ca and HandDryersCanada.

## Voice and positioning
Lead with “Save fresh drinking water. Simplify your washroom.” Explain gravity drainage, the MB Active odour trap, routine cleaning and periodic replacement plainly. Write natural Canadian English and fluent Canadian French. Avoid em dashes, generic AI phrasing, exaggerated superlatives, guaranteed savings, unsupported exclusivity, competitor conspiracies or maintenance-free claims. Zero flushing water does not mean zero cleaning or zero operating costs. Do not publish internal costs or profit margins. Treat historical pricing as unapproved until confirmed.

## Visual system
Use a refined architectural style: warm off-white, deep forest green, restrained lime accents, generous typography and real product photography. Mobile first, accessible, fast and readable. Each substantial section should eventually have a different relevant image, technical illustration or video. Never repeat stock photographs across the site. Prefer verified user-owned installation and product assets. Clearly identify conceptual illustrations; never fabricate installations, testimonials or customer logos. Track asset source, permission, alt text and page assignment. Do not distort product photography.
Create five original companion-logo concepts for review: (1) water droplet and Canadian maple cue, (2) U monogram, (3) architectural wordmark, (4) circular conservation symbol, (5) minimal Canadian signature. Do not alter the manufacturer's official URIMAT trademark or imply approval. Use a neutral typographic treatment until selection.

## Calculator specification
All values editable; illustrative defaults visibly labelled. Inputs: number of urinals, uses per urinal per operating day, operating days/year, litres/flush, marginal combined water and wastewater CAD/m³, annual existing maintenance CAD/site, annual waterless consumables and service CAD/site, installed conversion cost CAD/site.
Annual avoided flush water L = count × uses/day × days/year × L/flush.
Gross avoided utility cost = litres / 1000 × marginal combined rate.
Net annual operating difference = gross utility saving + existing maintenance - waterless consumables/service.
Simple payback years = installed cost / positive net annual savings. If savings are zero or negative, show “No positive payback under these assumptions.” State exclusions: financing, taxes, fixed utility charges, cleaning water and future changes. Cap days at 366, reject negative inputs, format CAD and locale correctly. Never describe illustrative defaults as municipal rates or guaranteed results.
